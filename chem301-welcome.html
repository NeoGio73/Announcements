Hello and welcome

Welcome to CHEM301, Biochemistry. My name is Professor John Picione and I will be your instructor for the next five weeks in this course. I am looking forward to our time together over the next few weeks.

You will discuss a variety of principles in biochemistry, which can be quite diverse. All fields of chemistry can be complex at times, but know that I will always try to help you as best as I can along the way — please never hesitate to reach out to me.

A word about pacing

This can be a challenging class, as all chemistry courses tend to be. It is very difficult to catch up if you fall behind. It is essential that you plan your time appropriately each week to complete your reading and assignments.

Reaching me

The best way to reach me is emailing me through Canvas. I will respond to any messages as quickly as possible, typically within 24 hours. I am on Eastern Time, and I typically check messages early in the morning and then again at night.

If you would like to meet via Teams, please don't hesitate to send me some of your available times via Canvas. Mondays and Fridays are best for me, but I do have some time slots free Tuesday through Thursday.

Getting around the course

I encourage you to look around the course and get acquainted with it as soon as you gain access. Each week in the course has a variety of items listed under Modules. I will post an announcement with some reminders and a short video to give you an overview of what you will have to tackle each week.

Your part: as a student, it is your responsibility to read the announcements and always follow what is posted in the Modules link each week.

Questions & support

If you need to contact any other of the university's services and you can't find that contact info, let me know and I will try to locate a contact for you. If you have any questions, please let me know that as well.

I wish you all the best, and here's to a great five weeks. Thank you, and I hope you enjoy this class.

Prof. John Picione
Instructor, CHEM301 Biochemistry
